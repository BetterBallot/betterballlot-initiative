Statement of Purpose

Student-led. Fact-driven. Nonpartisan.

We're a group of New Jersey high schoolers who think Board of Education elections should produce boards that actually reflect their districts. We research the mechanics, build the case, and show up at meetings to make it happen — because local democracy shouldn't depend on who happens to know the rules.

What Drives Us

Our values.

Advocacy

Showing Up

We send students to Board of Education meetings so that those in power can see what the people they represent truly want.

Nonpartisanship

Rules, Not Sides

Focused entirely on fair democratic processes, clear rules, and student representation.

Civic Literacy

Clear Voter Education

Clear, accessible voter education for communities.
