Why It Matters

What ranked-choice voting fixes.

01 / Representation

True Community Representation

Ensures school board members reflect the broad consensus of the community rather than a narrow faction in low-turnout elections.

02 / Choice

Eliminating Vote Splitting

Voters can rank aligned candidates freely without fearing they will split the vote and hand a seat to an unrepresentative candidate.

03 / Coalition

Issue-Focused Campaigns

Encourages candidates to build broad coalitions across the entire district rather than appealing only to a narrow base.

On the Ground

Recent events and community action.

Our organizers present at BoE meetings, libraries, PTO meetings, and classrooms. Every session is nonpartisan and open to the public.

Civic Outreach

YMCA Youth & Government Conference Tabling

Engaged over 90 student delegates on the mechanics of ranked-choice voting using an interactive fast-food ballot simulation to demonstrate multi-round tabulation and eliminate vote-splitting.

Monroe Township, NJ
Community Workshop

Monroe Township Community Presentation

Delivered an in-depth informational session to approximately 30 local residents, demonstrating how proportional RCV can bring fair, majority-backed representation to local Board of Education races.

Civic Engagement

Discussion with NJ Lt. Governor Dr. Dale G. Caldwell

Connected with NJ Lieutenant Governor Dr. Dale G. Caldwell at the YMCA YAG Conference to share our mission and talk through how ranked-choice voting could work in New Jersey school districts.
